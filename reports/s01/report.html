Method and evaluation protocol

Frozen encoder, patient bags

Audio is converted to mono at 16 kHz. Each recording supplies up to 5 deterministic 5.0000-second waveform windows. Valid encoder frames are mean-pooled within each window, then window embeddings are averaged equally into one recording representation. Padding masks exclude padded waveform samples and encoder frames; the window cap can leave gaps in very long recordings.

A frozen multilingual XLS-R speech encoder provides recording representations. A trainable LayerNorm → Linear(256) → GELU → Dropout(0.3) projection precedes patient-bag aggregation and a three-class linear diagnosis head. All available recording representations belong to that patient's bag.

Variant A uses mean MIL. Variant B uses learned attention MIL. Variant C uses the same attention MIL with train-only precomputed channel variants: each training recording can use its unaugmented base representation or an augmented representation. Validation and test use base representations.

Patient-primary selection

Five-fold CV combines original training and validation patients as the development pool. All recordings from one person stay together; A/B/C share identical folds and original test patients remain excluded. Mean CV patient macro F1 selects A/B/C; ties use lower mean patient loss, then A → B → C. The selected final model is fitted on original training patients; original validation patients select its checkpoint, followed by one original-test evaluation.

Training uses ordinary shuffled patient batches and inverse training-patient class-frequency weights in cross-entropy. Validation loss is unweighted patient cross-entropy. Single-instance recording results are supplemental and do not tune the bag model, select variants, or select checkpoints.

Interpretation and source confounding

Test class supports can be very small; inspect the per-class patient counts. Recording observations within a patient are correlated. A high class AUC on this holdout does not establish clinical generalization.

s00 test results were already known before s01 design. Comparisons on this reused holdout are exploratory and are not a fresh blinded confirmatory test. Validation and CV results used for model selection are not independent test estimates.

Channel transforms do not establish domain invariance. Cross-device or cross-site generalization requires independent devices or sites with diagnosis overlap. Sample rate, duration and encoding are available technical metadata; diagnosis must never be treated as a measured device identity.
